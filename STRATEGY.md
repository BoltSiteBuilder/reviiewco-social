# ReviiewCo social strategy: lead with value

Set by Rob on Oct 28, 2026. Every post gives the owner something useful first. The offer comes second.
Pattern: **here's how to do it yourself → most owners don't have time → we do it for you for $297/month.**

## Who we're talking to (customer avatar)

This is a working profile drawn from Rob's direction and the site's messaging. It is not survey data.

**Who he is.** He owns a home service business in one of our 22 trades (HVAC, plumbing, roofing, landscaping and the rest), usually in the Upstate SC / Charlotte area. He has 1 to 15 trucks and still works in the field himself. He's good at the work and proud of it. His phone is in his pocket while he's on a ladder, under a sink or on a roof. He runs jobs out of Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan, FieldEdge, or a spreadsheet and a notebook. He checks Facebook at lunch and after dinner, not at a desk.

**Pains (what keeps happening)**
- The guy across town has more reviews and gets the call, even though his work isn't better.
- Hundreds of happy customers over the years, and almost nothing on Google to show for it.
- Asking for a review face to face is awkward, so nobody on the crew does it.
- No time. Marketing is the first thing that stops when the schedule fills up.
- Missed calls while he's on a job, and those callers dial the next contractor.
- His last review was months ago, so his profile looks quiet next to busy competitors.
- He loses bids to lowballers when he's competing on price instead of trust.
- His website is an old business card (or he doesn't have one) and doesn't bring in calls.

**Fears (what he worries could happen)**
- One bad review tanks his rating.
- Doing something that gets his Google profile in trouble (review gating, incentives, the April 2026 rule changes).
- Getting locked into a long contract for marketing that doesn't produce.
- Getting burned by an agency again: big promises, a dashboard he never opens, no calls.
- Falling behind as homeowners start asking ChatGPT and Google's AI for contractors.
- Losing control of his business: who owns the Google profile, what gets said in his name.

**Likes**
- Straight talk from someone local who respects the trade.
- Things that just work without him babysitting them.
- Word of mouth, referrals and proof he can see.
- One price, no surprises, month to month.
- Staying in control: his profile, his approval, his customers.

**Dislikes**
- Sales pitches, jargon, "growth hacks," anything that sounds like an agency.
- Apps to learn and dashboards to check.
- Contracts, setup fees, per-text charges, fine print.
- Fake reviews and anyone who games the system.
- Being talked down to.

## Caption formula
1. **Hook** that names a pain or fear in his words (first line, under ~12 words).
2. **Value**: the tip, the number, the step. Useful even if he never buys.
3. **DIY or done-for-you**: "You can do this yourself. Most owners don't have time. That's what we're for."
4. **CTA, value first**: "Comment GOLD (or DM us GOLD) and we'll send you the free playbook." Then: "Rather hand it off? $297/month, no contract. Book 30 min with Rob: reviiewco.com/book" or "Start at reviiewco.com/start".
5. 6–10 hashtags.

Stories (Instagram) can't take comments, so their image says **"DM us 'KEYWORD'"** and the caption isn't shown.
